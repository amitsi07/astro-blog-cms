## The Concept & Lighting Architecture

This portrait prompt is calibrated specifically for **Midjourney v6.0** and **Flux.1 Dev** to eliminate artificial plastic skin rendering. By specifying a prime 85mm focal length and directional golden hour rim lighting, the diffusion engine calculates real optical bokeh rather than synthetic blurs.

> **Pro Tip for Photographers:** Always add `--style raw` when using Midjourney v6 to disable Midjourney's default saturation boost. This retains authentic micro-skin pores and natural subsurface scattering.

---

### Key Formula & Parameters:
1. **Camera Angle:** Eye-level 85mm medium portrait
2. **Key Lighting:** Low-angle 2800K warm rim light + diffused 5000K fill
3. **Texture Anchor:** Natural micro-freckles, subtle skin pore fidelity
4. **Style Parameter:** `--style raw --v 6.0 --ar 3:4`

### Recommended Variables
- **Time of Day:** Golden Hour, Dusk, Blue Hour, Dawn
- **Lens:** 85mm f/1.8, 50mm f/1.2, 105mm macro
- **Wardrobe:** Textured beige wool sweater, raw silk scarf, structured trench coat
