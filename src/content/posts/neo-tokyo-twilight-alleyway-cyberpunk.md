## Achieving True Cyberpunk Atmosphere Without Neon Slop

Most cyberpunk prompts fall into the trap of oversaturated magenta and cyan blurs. This prompt introduces damp atmospheric mist, 35mm optical chromatic aberration, and authentic Japanese kanji typography to anchor the scene in real cinematic world-building.

:::warning
**Avoid High CFG Scales:** Setting CFG scale too high (above 8.0 in SDXL or 4.5 in Flux) will blow out the neon highlights into solid white blocks. Keep highlight roll-off smooth.
:::

---

### Camera & Atmosphere Setup:

- **Lens:** 35mm anamorphic prime, f/2.0 aperture

- **Atmosphere:** Twilight drizzle, micro-water puddles, suspended humid mist

- **Color Grading:** Teal-orange contrast with Kodak Vision3 500T 5219 film stock simulation

:::info
**Recommended Aspect Ratio:** `--ar 16:9` or `--ar 21:9` for anamorphic widescreen panorama.
:::
