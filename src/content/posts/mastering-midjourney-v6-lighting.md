# Complete Guide to Optical AI Prompt Engineering

When generating photorealistic scenes with **Astro Content Collections** and AI diffusion models, standard descriptive adjectives like "ultra detailed" or "hyperrealistic" often hurt prompt quality. Instead, use real photographic nomenclature.

## 1. Color Temperature in Kelvins
- **2000K - 2800K:** Golden Hour, Candlelight, Sunset warm glow
- **3200K:** Tungsten studio halogen lights
- **4500K - 5500K:** Clean noon daylight
- **6500K - 8000K:** Overcast sky, cool twilight, blue hour

## 2. Lens Selection & Perspective Compression
- **24mm - 35mm:** Environmental portraits, expansive architectural context, wide establishing shots.
- **50mm:** Natural human eye perspective, documentary and candid street shots.
- **85mm - 105mm:** Flattering face proportions, creamy bokeh falloff, editorial fashion.

> **Key Rule:** By specifying the exact camera lens (e.g. `Hasselblad HC 85mm f/1.8`), you anchor the diffusion engine's latent space to real medium format optical physics.
