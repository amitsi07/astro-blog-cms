## Creating Authentic Rain & Wet Pavement Reflections

Cyberpunk aesthetics often suffer from muddy neon smears. To get clean, sharp specular highlights in rain puddles, notice how this prompt introduces two critical optical anchors: **volumetric steam** and **Cooke Anamorphic T2.3**.

> **Pro Tip:** Prompting for "Cooke Anamorphic" forces the model to render authentic horizontal lens flares and elliptical bokeh rather than generic spherical light spots.

---

### Optical Setup
- **Focal Length:** 40mm Anamorphic
- **Aperture:** T2.3 wide open
- **Color Palette:** Magenta `#ff007f`, Electric Cyan `#00f3ff`, Deep Charcoal `#0e1017`
- **Surface Texture:** Wet asphalt with micro water droplets
