## Worldbuilding Through Light & Atmospheric Scale

To create true cinematic depth in fantasy scenes, always establish three distinct visual planes:
1. **Foreground:** Overhanging stone vines or rock formations giving scale.
2. **Midground:** The floating island monolith with detailed architectural elements.
3. **Background:** Distant cloud layers and celestial lighting gradients.

> **Prompt Engineering Secret:** Roger Deakins lighting cues produce subtle naturalistic rim light on stone textures rather than artificial video game glows.
